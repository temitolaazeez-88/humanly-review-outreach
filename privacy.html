Privacy

How HumanlyReview handles your information

We use the information needed to verify customers, process reviews, provide support, deliver reports and operate the service. We design the support workflow so that highly sensitive backend secrets and customer document contents are not exported to the AI-support knowledge sheets.

Last updated: 26 September 2026. This page describes the current HumanlyReview service configuration. If a feature changes materially, this notice should be updated.

Information we may process

Depending on how you use HumanlyReview, this can include your email address, order and ticket references, document metadata used for pricing and safety checks, documents you submit after the required payment or complimentary-access step, assignment instructions you choose to provide, payment status and transaction references, support messages, delivery activity, affiliate information and marketing preferences.

Your documents

Before payment, the selected document stays on your device while the website reads only the local file details needed for pricing and safety checks. After verified payment or eligible complimentary submission, the submitted document enters the controlled review workflow. Customer document contents are not published as part of the service and are not intentionally exported to the HumanlyReview AI Support Brain or AI Support Desk.

Payments

Payments are handled through the configured payment provider. HumanlyReview stores the transaction references and payment status needed to reconcile an order. Do not send card numbers, PINs or banking passwords through the support form.

Support and AI-assisted customer care

When you contact support, HumanlyReview stores the conversation under a ticket reference. The service may use AI-assisted support tools to help prepare factual customer-service replies. The support integration is designed to use a separate sanitized support workspace: customer emails are masked there by default, document contents and private Drive file identifiers are excluded, and common sensitive strings are redacted. Human review or escalation may still be required for payment disputes, refunds, privacy or security concerns, legal issues and unusual cases.

Service providers

HumanlyReview relies on service providers that may process information as necessary to operate the service, including Google services for hosting, spreadsheets, file storage and email delivery; the configured payment provider for payment processing; and, when the owner uses the AI-assisted support workflow, OpenAI/ChatGPT for assistance with the sanitized support information made available through the dedicated support connection. Each provider operates under its own terms and privacy practices.

Marketing

Promotional emails are sent only to leads recorded as actively opted in under the current HumanlyReview marketing setting. Every campaign is designed to include an unsubscribe option. Unsubscribing from marketing does not stop transactional messages needed for an order, delivery, support ticket or other service request.

Retention and deletion

Delivered files are retained for the configured delivery-retention window and are then eligible for cleanup. Some order, payment, support and audit records may be retained longer where needed for service continuity, fraud prevention, dispute handling, accounting, security or legal obligations. HumanlyReview also provides archive/trash controls for operational content such as campaigns and updates before permanent deletion.

Security

HumanlyReview uses server-side verification for sensitive operations and keeps backend secrets out of the public website code. No online system can promise absolute security. Please avoid sending information that is not needed for your review or support request.

Your choices

  • You can decline optional marketing.
  • You can unsubscribe from promotional emails using the link in a campaign.
  • You can contact support about an order, privacy question or correction request.
  • You remain responsible for keeping your own original copy of every document you submit.

Questions

For privacy or account questions, use the HumanlyReview Help Centre. For organisation-level enquiries, contact hello@humanlyreview.com.
