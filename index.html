Questions

Good to know.

Will you write or edit my work?

No. HumanlyReview reviews your work and identifies exactly what to correct. The writing stays yours.

What can I submit?

Academic work such as theses, dissertations, essays, coursework and assignments, plus professional and technical documents across every field.

Do I need my assignment brief?

It is optional but recommended. Adding the question, brief or marking criteria lets us assess your work against what was asked.

How is my price set?

Automatically, from the size of your files. Multiple files are combined into one price, and you see it before you pay.

How do I receive my report?

You are notified when it is ready. Track your submission at any time and download your report, typically as PDF and DOCX.
